About Nuevo

Crafted by
One Expert Hand

Nuevo is a premium mobile car detailing service based in Beirut, founded and operated by Anthony Nassif. Every job is handled personally — no delegation, no shortcuts.

We bring the full studio experience directly to your driveway. Whether it's a daily driver or a supercar, every vehicle receives the same obsessive attention to detail that Anthony has built his reputation on.

From a full exterior and interior detail to multi-stage paint corrections and ceramic coatings — if it involves your car's finish, we do it right.

What We Offer

Our Services

01
Exterior Detailing
Thorough wash, clay bar treatment, iron decontamination, and hand-dried finish. Your paint returns to its factory brilliance.
02
Interior Detailing
Deep vacuum, steam cleaning, leather conditioning, glass polishing, and odour elimination. A spotless cabin inside and out.
03
Ceramic Coating
Professional-grade nano-ceramic protection that bonds to your paint, providing years of hydrophobic shield against UV, dirt, and scratches.
04
Paint Correction
Multi-stage machine polishing to eliminate swirl marks, scratches, oxidation, and water spots — restoring true depth and clarity.
05
Anti-Fog Treatment
Specialist coating applied to interior glass surfaces to prevent fogging — ensuring crystal-clear visibility in all weather conditions.
06
Full Detail Package
The complete Nuevo experience — exterior, interior, engine bay, tyres, and glass — everything in one visit for a showroom-ready result.
